# Minimum Wage Calculator UK — National Living Wage 2026

> The National Living Wage is £12.71 an hour for workers aged 21 and over. At 37.5 hours a week that is £24,784.50 a year, or £21,364.44 after tax.

Reference notes for the UK tax year **2026/27**, computed from HMRC's
published rates rather than copied from another site.

## How it works

| Age | Rate an hour |
|---|---:|
| 21 and over | £12.71 |
| 18 to 20 | £10.85 |
| Under 18 and apprentices | £8.00 |

Salaried workers can still be underpaid. If your annual salary divided by the hours you actually work falls below the rate for your age, your employer is in breach regardless of what the contract says.

Unpaid time counts: mandatory training, security checks, and time spent travelling between appointments all form part of working time.

## What this does not cover

Accommodation offset, salary sacrifice and deductions for uniform can all pull effective pay below the minimum. Sacrifice cannot legally take you below it.

## Source and tax year

Figures are for the 2026/27 tax year, which runs 6 April 2026
to 5 April 2027, and were checked against
[gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027) on 28 August 2026.

Rates change every April, and often at a Budget. Check the tax year a figure
belongs to before relying on it.
